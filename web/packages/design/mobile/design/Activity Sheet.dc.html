Accessibility and contrast, both themes
A modal dialog. role=dialog, aria-modal; the label is one sentence — "Sent, −$10.00, Completed". Focus moves to the sheet on open (the title, then Share, Close) and returns to the row on close. Close is always present at 44 pt; the VoiceOver escape gesture, Escape on the web and a scrim tap all dismiss.
Nothing needs the drag. The Transaction details row is the non-drag path to full height — it announces "Transaction details, collapsed" and, when activated at half height, both expands the sheet and opens the section. Dragging is a shortcut to the same two states.
Reduce motion: open and close are a 200 ms fade, no slide; detent changes jump with a 120 ms opacity dip; no skeleton pulse.
Contrast as in the Activity handoff: secondary text fgMuted (7.6:1 light / 9.9:1 dark), failed in brick (4.9 / 7.0), FAILED solid, IconTile soft fills ≥ 3:1. The 40 % scrim only dims the feed; the sheet's own surface is unaffected.
Copy: addresses 4…4 in mono, never uppercased; − is U+2212; "View on ThruScan" replaces "View in explorer" and opens scan.thru.org/tx/<signature>?network=alphanet.
