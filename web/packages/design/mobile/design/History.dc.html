Contrast and copy calls, both themes
fgSubtle is 2.5:1 on light (#81A7A7 on #F9FBFB) — sublines, times and day labels use fgMuted (7.6:1 / 9.9:1 dark); fgSubtle stays on inactive tab tints only.
Error red (#FF4750) is 3.4:1 on light — Failed uses destructive brick (4.9:1 light, 7.0:1 dark); the kit's validation red stays on fields.
Badge brand tone is 3.9:1 (brick on brickSoft) — FAILED is a solid badge, brick fill with bg text (4.9:1 / 7.3:1) → Badge tone error, new. PENDING keeps the kit's warn tone (5.6:1 / 8.0:1).
IconTile soft fills carry glyphs only — forest on forestSoft 5.3:1 light / 3.3:1 dark, saffron on saffronSoft 5.6 / 8.0, brick on brickSoft 3.9 / 4.3; all ≥ 3:1.
Addresses are never uppercased — base58 is case-sensitive, and the old caps meta line ("TA9XPL…3KQ1") printed a different string from the one you could copy. Shortened as 4 + … + 4 in mono.
A row is one sentence to a screen reader: "Received $25.00 from ta7Q…x2Lp, 2:14 PM" · "Sent $10.00 to taB3…9kDe, pending, 2:16 PM". Children are aria-hidden; the row is the control.
