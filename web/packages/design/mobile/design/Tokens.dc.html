Rules the implementation enforces
Home shows four rows: THRUSD, then the next three by ticker A→Z; VIEW ALL → appears only when more exist and opens the Tokens screen. Home never scrolls at 844 — Recent activity drops to two rows when the list is full.
Order: THRUSD first, then by ticker; duplicates keep mint order; "Unknown token" last.
No prices: the THRUSD row shows "56 THRUSD" under the title and $56.00 on the right — the only dollar figure. Other rows show the mint under the title and the exact amount on the right, in the token's own decimals (up to 9), thousands separators, no trailing zeros.
Amounts never truncate. The title ellipsises first; at Dynamic Type XL a row whose amount exceeds 60 % of its width grows and stacks the amount under the title.
Duplicate tickers are told apart by the mint subtitle (taZt…9Kq2 vs taQm…7xRw); the accessible name includes the mint; no ticker is renamed.
Frozen and unsupported tokens keep a live row (neutral Badge) and open their screen; only Send is disabled, with the reason under the button pair. Unknown decimals show the raw integer with "units".
Accessible names: rows read "THRUSD, $56.00, open" (duplicates and unknowns add the mint; badges add "frozen" / "unsupported"); VIEW ALL → is "View all tokens"; the explainer is a button with aria-expanded; a disabled Send is aria-disabled and described by its reason.
QR is dark on light in both themes: #181B1B modules on #F9FBFB, 1 px #B6CECE border, EC level H, Thru mark 32 knocked out on a 3 px pad.
Open questions
No chevron on the rows, as in today's Balances design — the whole row presses (bgMuted) and VIEW ALL → signals the list goes on. Add the kit Cell chevron if testing shows people don't tap rows.
"Spendable" vs "Balance" on the token screen: Spendable is honest when other accounts hold more; if that case is rare, "Balance" reads simpler.
Network badge in the header (Alphanet / Betanet, forest dot) follows the Settings Network row. Drop if the existing header label already covers it.
Receive for THRUSD opens the Deposit sheet with USDC on Solana pre-selected. Confirm that over card as the default.
